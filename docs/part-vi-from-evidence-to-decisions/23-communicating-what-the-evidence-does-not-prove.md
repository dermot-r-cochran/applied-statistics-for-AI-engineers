## Key questions

- What is supported by the evidence, and what remains unknown?
- Which assumptions would break the conclusion if they fail?
- How can the report stay usable for decision-makers without overselling certainty?


## Engineering interpretation

Every evaluation report should include a claims-and-limits section, decision implications, and the next evidence needed to reduce key uncertainties.

Project Frog report templates use synthetic examples to demonstrate how to communicate strong caveats without making the work sound indecisive or unusable.
