## Key questions

- What magnitude of change would justify action?
- Could a statistically significant result still be operationally trivial?
- Would equivalence or non-inferiority better match the decision than superiority testing?


## Engineering interpretation

Document the smallest improvement or degradation that matters, then judge the observed effect against that threshold rather than against significance alone.
