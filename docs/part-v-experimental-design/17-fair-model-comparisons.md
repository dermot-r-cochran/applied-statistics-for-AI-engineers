## Key questions

- Were both systems evaluated on the same underlying opportunities for success and failure?
- Are the labels, scoring rules, and thresholds held constant?
- Which differences in environment or preprocessing limit the conclusion?


## Engineering interpretation

Publish a comparability ruling with every benchmark comparison. If conditions differ, say whether the result is direct evidence, provisional evidence, or not comparable.
