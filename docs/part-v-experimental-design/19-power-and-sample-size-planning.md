## Key questions

- How much evidence is needed to detect the minimum meaningful effect?
- How do baseline performance and dependence structure change the requirement?
- What happens if the collected sample falls short of the plan?


## Engineering interpretation

Plan sample sizes with explicit assumptions and decision contingencies. If the design is clustered or subgroup-critical, row counts alone are insufficient planning units.
