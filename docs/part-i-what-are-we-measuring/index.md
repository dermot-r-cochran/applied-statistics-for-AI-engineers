# Part I: What Are We Measuring?

Measurement starts by defining the system outcome, the observable proxies, and the limits of any reference signal used for evaluation.

## What this part does

This part establishes the main questions, failure modes, and decision responsibilities for the chapters that follow.

## Chapters in this part

- [1. The Metric Is Not the System](01-the-metric-is-not-the-system.md) — Metrics summarize selected outcomes, but they never capture the full system, the operating environment, or the downstream consequences of being wrong.
- [2. From Concepts to Measurements](02-from-concepts-to-measurements.md) — Teams move from abstract ideas like reliability or trustworthiness to measurements by specifying events, units, labels, and decision thresholds.
- [3. Ground Truth Is a Measurement Too](03-ground-truth-is-a-measurement-too.md) — Reference labels are produced by people, guidelines, tools, and compromises, so they deserve the same scrutiny as model outputs.

## Throughline

Project Frog reappears across these chapters so ideas connect through one synthetic workflow rather than isolated examples.
